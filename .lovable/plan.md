- My ownership and cross-functional process

- Outcomes, evidence, and next measurement steps

Only verified facts from the supplied newsletters and existing case-study material will be stated as shipped outcomes. Metrics without supplied measured values will be labeled as success criteria or KPIs to monitor, not presented as achieved results.
